Your Top 3 Priorities at Level 2
These are the three moves that create the most leverage at your level.
1
Document your 3 most critical processes.
Pick the three recurring tasks that consume the most time or cause the most inconsistency. Write each one in six parts: title, purpose, owner, trigger, steps, and output. Write them for someone who has never worked with you — specific enough that they could execute on day one without asking you a question. If the documentation requires your personal explanation to make sense, it is not done.
2
Run the handoff test.
Give each documented process to a real person on your team. Watch them try to execute it without asking you anything. Every question they ask is a gap in the document. Fix the gaps. Run the test again. The document is done when someone new can follow it cold and produce the output you would have produced yourself.
3
Audit your pricing.
Revenue is growing but margins feel tighter — because the team costs money before it produces it. You are subsidizing growth with personal overwork and have not updated pricing to reflect the real cost of delivery. List every offer. Calculate what it actually costs to deliver each one — time, team, tools. If your margin depends on you personally doing the work at below-market rates, your pricing is broken.

The reason nobody does it as well as you is not because they lack capability. It is because the standard only exists in your head. You have not written it down. You have not trained to it. You are the standard — which means the standard leaves every time you do.
A well-built system is more reliable than any individual. Until your processes are documented, your team’s performance depends entirely on your personal presence. That is the ceiling. Documentation is how you remove it.
 
The Cost of Staying
Every year you stay at Level 2:
— You add headcount but not freedom — the team grows, your hours do not shrink
— Every new hire becomes a 3–6 month personal investment of your time to train
— When someone leaves, they take institutional knowledge with them
— You cannot grow without being present — and presence has a hard limit
— You are building a team that depends on you instead of a business that runs
Another year at Level 2 means another year where every hire makes you busier, not freer. The team gets bigger. The bottleneck stays the same size.
