The framework measures nine distinct areas of your business. Each one operates at its own level, with its own bottleneck and its own fix. Here is what each P actually measures:
Pipeline — Can your business create demand on purpose, or does it depend on your personal hustle?
Profit — Do you have a clear, predictable picture of where revenue comes from and where it goes?
Perspective — What belief is keeping you at this level instead of the next one?
Principles — Does your team know how to make decisions without asking you?
Program — Before you hire a person, have you asked whether a system or tool can handle the job?
People — Are the right people in the right roles, operating without your constant direction?
Process — Does the playbook exist in writing, or does it live in your head?
Progress — Are you measuring the right things early enough to act on them?
Power — Who makes which decisions, and are the right ones going to the right people?
