What the Quick Assessment Measures
The Quick Assessment scored your business across five categories:
1. Foundation & Clarity — Do you have a documented plan, clear revenue targets, and a defined pipeline for creating business on purpose?
2. Systems & Execution — Are your core processes documented? Is delivery consistent regardless of who handles it? Does automation handle the repeatable work?
3. Team & Autonomy — Can your team operate without you for a week? Do they make decisions independently? Do they improve processes without being asked?
4. Leadership & Authority — Are you coaching, not just managing? Is decision authority distributed clearly? Does the team hold itself to the standard?
5. The Strategist — Are you working on the business, not in it? Is your calendar structured for strategic work? Does the business improve when you step back?
These five categories map to the 9 P’s — Pipeline, Profit, Perspective, Principles, Program, People, Process, Progress, and Power — which is where the In-Depth Assessment goes deeper.
 
The Quick Snapshot vs. the Full Picture
The Quick Assessment shows your overall level. It tells you where you are on the map.
What it does not show is which specific P is your primary constraint — the one area that, if fixed, creates movement in everything else. It does not show which P is your superpower. It does not give you per-P levels, per-P bottlenecks, or per-P next moves.
The In-Depth Assessment breaks your business into all 9 P’s — each with its own level, its own bottleneck, and its own specific next step. It takes about 8 minutes. And it gives you the complete diagnostic — not just the coordinate, but the entire map.
